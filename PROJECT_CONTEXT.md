\# PROJECT VISION



Bhraman Saathi is a smart tourism ecosystem designed to connect tourists with:



\- Hotels

\- Travel agencies

\- Local tourism businesses

\- Local experiences

\- Destinations



The goal is to improve the tourist experience while increasing visibility, customers and opportunities for local tourism businesses.



Bhraman Saathi should NOT look like a simple hotel booking website.



The core idea is:



AI + Personalized Travel + Local Tourism + Hidden Gems + Business Empowerment



\---



\# KEY DIFFERENTIATORS



\## 1. AI-Powered Personalized Trip Planning



Tourists can provide:



\- Destination

\- Budget

\- Number of days

\- Number of people

\- Interests

\- Travel style



The platform will eventually generate a personalized itinerary.



For the current frontend prototype, this will use mock/static data.



\---



\## 2. Local Business Matching



Bhraman Saathi should connect tourists with:



\- Hotels

\- Homestays

\- Local guides

\- Restaurants

\- Local experiences

\- Handicraft businesses

\- Travel services



The long-term goal is to increase visibility and business opportunities for local tourism providers.



\---



\## 3. Hidden Gem Discovery



The platform should encourage tourists to explore lesser-known destinations instead of recommending only highly popular tourist locations.



Example destinations:



\- Daringbadi

\- Satkosia

\- Raghurajpur

\- Bhitarkanika



Odisha will be used heavily in the prototype and SIH demonstration.



\---



\## 4. Tourism Business Analytics



Tourism businesses will eventually have dashboards showing:



\- Profile views

\- Bookings

\- Revenue

\- Customer interest

\- Ratings

\- Tourism demand



This functionality is currently only a frontend prototype.



\---



\## 5. Sustainable Tourism



A future version may consider:



\- Tourist crowd levels

\- Local business contribution

\- Distance

\- Environmental impact

\- Seasonality



The goal is to encourage responsible and distributed tourism.
